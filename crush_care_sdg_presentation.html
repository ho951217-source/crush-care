Research Motivation

Exploring the gap between subjective feelings and objective message behavior

1

Higher needs, higher misreading

The stronger the desire for love and belonging, the easier it is to over-interpret neutral messages.

2

Perception ≠ Reality

"They replied with a heart — they must like me" vs. what data actually shows.

3

CRUSH CARE brings rationality back

Crush Index measures your emotional heat.
Message Temperature measures their interaction heat.

Core Goal: Detect the gap between "what you think" and "what the data shows" → Help users see the difference between emotional investment and actual messaging behavior.

Module 2 — Message Temperature

Quantifying their interaction heat through word count and reply speed

Word Count → Temperature

Cold — avg. < 10 chars     (no real engagement)
Normal — 10–19 chars    (polite but neutral)
Hot — avg. ≥ 20 chars     (willing to invest time)

Reply Time → Speed

⏳ Slow   reply time > 3600 s (1 hr)  → chat is not a priority
⏱️ Normal   600–3600 s (10 min–1 hr)  → replies when free
⚡ Fast   reply time ≤ 600 s (10 min)  → highly attentive to you
⚠️

Danger Alert — Triggered When:

Crush Index > 75 AND Reply Time > 3600 s (1 hour) → "You're deeply invested but their engagement is clearly low. Suggestion: Reduce initiation, refocus on your own life before re-evaluating."

Results & Analysis

Comparing emotional investment vs. actual message behaviour across all three cases

User Crush Index Status Msg Temperature Reply Speed Pattern Alert
小A 30.00 Safe Hot (crush) Fast (60 s) Misaligned None
小B 60.00 Moderate Hot Normal (650 s) Mutual None
小C 92.00 Danger Cold Slow (4000 s) One-Sided ⚠️ Triggered

Finding 1 — Misaligned Ambiguity

When the user's Crush Index is low but the crush is highly warm, there is no emotional risk to the user. The system correctly withholds the danger alert.

Finding 2 — Balanced Mutual Attraction

When both message temperature and Crush Index are moderate and aligned, the relationship shows healthy reciprocal engagement — the healthiest pattern.

Finding 3 — One-Sided Burning

High Crush Index + Cold/Slow messages = the most psychologically damaging scenario. The system triggers an alert to protect the user's wellbeing.

Finding 4 — Data Over Intuition

Users in the "Head Over Heels" category often feel the connection is mutual. Objective message data breaks the cognitive bias and shows reality.

SDGs Connection

How CRUSH CARE contributes to the UN Sustainable Development Goals

💚

Primary Goal — SDG 3: Good Health & Well-Being

CRUSH CARE reduces ambiguity-induced anxiety by replacing "guessing and overthinking" with objective data. When users can see the numbers, emotional distress decreases — promoting more stable mental health in young people navigating romantic uncertainty.

SDG 4 — Quality Education (Emotional Literacy)

Helps users distinguish between genuine interest, politeness, and personal boundaries — building emotional intelligence that schools rarely teach.

SDG 5 — Gender Equality

Analysis is behavior-based, not gender-based. No stereotypes are applied — the system respects all users equally and emphasises mutual respect in relationships.

SDG 10 — Reduced Inequalities

Provides an anonymous, self-service emotional support tool accessible to students who lack the confidence or resources to seek external guidance.

Reflection & Future Development

Honest limitations and where CRUSH CARE can grow

Current LimitationFuture Improvement
Message temperature is highly subjective Add a tone-word library, emoji analysis, and a multi-variable scoring model
Poor performance on Chinese/non-English text Integrate Chinese tokenisation + sentiment analysis (e.g., jieba + CKIP)
Crush Index relies entirely on self-reported feelings Combine with behavioral data; create personalised adaptive scales; expand question types
Conclusions still require human interpretation Build an "ambiguity health score," trend graphs, and automated relationship advice engine
Limited dataset — analysis is narrow Expand dataset; add situational categories; analyse stickers and reaction emojis

Conclusion

What CRUSH CARE gives users — four key takeaways

1

Know how deep you're in

Understand whether you're genuinely interested or just caught in an emotional spiral of overthinking.

2

Separate fact from imagination

Identify which parts of the conversation are real signals and which are self-constructed narratives.

3

Recognise your emotional patterns

Understand the habits and blind spots you tend to fall into during ambiguous romantic situations.

4

Maintain rationality and boundaries

Prevent emotions from being amplified disproportionately — so you can stay grounded, protect yourself, and make clearer decisions.

CRUSH CARE turns ambiguous feelings into measurable data — because clarity is the first step toward emotional health.
